Interactive Phonics Lessons

Start with basic sounds and build your way up! Click on each sound to hear the pronunciation.

🎵 Lesson 1: The Letter "S" Sound

The letter S makes the sound: "sssss" (like a snake)

Words with S:

🐍 SAT - (s-a-t)
☀️ SUN - (s-u-n)
🪨 SAND - (s-a-n-d)

Practice: Say these words out loud slowly!

🎵 Lesson 2: The Letter "A" Sound

The letter A makes the sound: "aaa" (like an apple)

Words with A:

🍎 APPLE - (a-p-p-l-e)
🐜 ANT - (a-n-t)
🐈 CAT - (c-a-t)

Practice: Say these words out loud slowly!

🎵 Lesson 3: The Letter "T" Sound

The letter T makes the sound: "ttt" (like a clock tick)

Words with T:

🐯 TIGER - (t-i-g-e-r)
🌳 TREE - (t-r-e-e)
🚜 TAT - (t-a-t)

Practice: Say these words out loud slowly!

🎮 Blending Exercise: Combine Sounds

Now let's blend the sounds together!

Try blending:

S + A + T = SAT 🎉

Say each sound separately, then blend them together smoothly!
